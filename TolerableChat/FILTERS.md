# TolerableChat's filters: what they hide, and why

TolerableChat exists for one reason: to make public chat in WoW about WoW. Trade, General, LFG and
the world channels are where people find groups, sell things and ask where a quest giver is. They're
also where the same handful of arguments start every evening and take over every channel. This page
explains which topics TolerableChat hides, how firmly, and why. **[TERMS.md](TERMS.md)** lists every
built-in word and phrase.

## The idea in short

- **It hides topics, not opinions.** Every filter catches both sides of an argument. A message about
  politics is hidden whether it's for or against; so is a message about religion, whichever religion.
- **Hiding a topic isn't a judgement about it.** Most of what's on these lists isn't bad at all. It's
  just not the conversation TolerableChat's author wants to have in a video game, and in public chat
  it almost always turns into an argument.
- **It errs on the side of hiding.** A word filter can't read intent. It can't tell a friendly
  mention from a jab, or a sincere question from bait. So the lists are deliberately broad: missing a
  harmless message costs you little, while letting the arguments through costs the whole channel.
  Every category, and every single word, can be turned down or off (see the end of this page).
- **Your own circles are left alone.** Your guild, party, raid and friends are never filtered, and
  whispers aren't word-filtered by default. Personal conversations stay yours.

## How firmly: the four choices

Every category is set to one of these on the Filter tab:

| Choice | What happens | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Mute Forever** | That player is muted: everything they say is hidden from then on | Hostility toward people: slurs and bigotry |
| **Timeout** | The message is hidden, and so is the rest of that player's non-game chat for 30 minutes before and after (the Timeout Duration) | Topics that start arguments: politics, religion, nationality, streamers, crude talk; and harassment |
| **Hide Only** | Just that message is hidden | Tired jokes (Dead Horses) |
| **Off** | The category isn't checked | Nothing, by default |

The timeout is what makes a hidden topic stay hidden. When someone posts about an election, their
replies, follow-ups and "lol ok boomer"s for the next half hour go too, so you don't see half an
argument. Game talk gets through even during a timeout: group requests, class and role words,
dungeon names, item and profession links, and WTS/WTB posts (unless the sender is spamming).

Mute Forever is kept for slurs and bigotry. Anything that is only a topic, however heated, gets a
timeout, so nobody is muted for good for mentioning a subject. So does telling someone to kill
themselves, which many players say as banter: the timeout still takes the exchange with it.

## Why LGBT, gender and identity topics are on the list

This is the part people ask about, so it deserves a straight answer.

Words like *gay*, *queer*, *lesbian*, *trans*, *LGBT*, *pronouns*, *nonbinary*, *bisexual* and
*sexuality*, the argument words *homophobic* and *transphobic*, and gender-politics phrases like
"gender ideology", are hidden in public chat. **That's not because any of them is bad, or because
anyone's identity is a problem.** It's because in WoW's public channels these words almost never
arrive as a neutral mention. They come as an insult ("that's so gay"), as bait aimed at someone, or
as the opening move of a culture-war argument that runs for an hour. A word filter can't tell those
apart from a friendly "my gay brother plays a hunter too", so TolerableChat steps back from the whole
topic rather than try to referee it.

How firmly each is handled:

- **The topic words** (LGBT, gay, queer, lesbian, trans, pronouns, gender politics, homophobic,
  transphobic) are in **Politics**: the message is hidden and the sender gets a timeout, nothing
  more. Nobody is muted for mentioning who they are, or for calling something homophobic.
- **Words game chat mostly uses as insults**, *gay*, *homo*, *autistic* and *retarded* among them,
  and *dyke*, which lesbians have reclaimed, are in Politics too, so they get the same timeout.
  "That's so gay" and "my gay brother plays a hunter" are both hidden, and neither mutes anyone: the
  filter can't tell them apart, so it treats them the same and keeps it light.
- **Slurs, mocking names and hate slogans** (the anti-gay and anti-trans slurs, "alphabet people"
  and the like) are in **Bigotry**: Mute Forever. Those aren't topics; they're aimed at people.

**The cost, honestly.** While this is on, an LGBT player can't mention themselves in public chat
without being hidden from you, the same as someone mentioning their church or their country. That's
the price of a filter that can't read intent. If you'd rather see those mentions, switch off the
single words you want (Filter tab → Edit terms…, search "gay", untick it) instead of turning off all
of Politics.

The same reasoning covers the other identity topics: race and ethnicity ("white people", "black
people" and similar), religion, and nationality. None of these is hidden because it's offensive to
talk about. They're hidden because public game chat isn't where those conversations go well, and
they aren't what TolerableChat's author wants WoW to be about.

Your guild, your group and your friends are never filtered, so none of this touches the
conversations you choose to have.

## Category by category

### Streamers (Timeout)
Names of WoW and MMO streamers (Asmongold, xQc, Sodapoppin and the like), streamer events such as
OnlyFangs, and words like *streamer*, *twitch* and *youtuber*. Streamer drama spills into chat every
time something happens on stream, and it's never about the game you're playing.

### Politics (Timeout)
The biggest list, because it's where the most arguments start:
- **parties, ideologies and their labels**, from *democrat* and *republican* to *woke* in its
  political uses, *maga*, *antifa*, *socialism*, *libertarian*;
- **slang, insults and slogans** of every side (*libtard*, *magat*, *sjw*, *soyboy*, "fake news",
  "free speech", "white privilege");
- **politicians and pundits**, by full name where the surname is an ordinary word (*Ted Cruz*, not
  *cruz*);
- **world leaders**, **elections**, **institutions** and **news networks**;
- **issues**: immigration, abortion, guns, climate, covid and vaccines, drugs and cartels,
  healthcare, LGBT and gender topics (see above), race and racism, slavery and colonialism, Israel and
  Gaza, Ukraine, antisemitism, and crime topics that get thrown around in arguments;
- **plain Nazi mentions**: *nazi*, *hitler*, *holocaust*. A history question, "grammar nazi" or
  "Garrosh is basically orc Hitler" gets a timeout, not a mute. Praising them is different: see
  Bigotry.

### Nationality (Timeout)
Countries, continents and regions (including *EU* and *UK*), US states, the people from all of them
(*americans*, *texans*, *europeans*), ethnic labels, mild nicknames such as *gringo* and *kraut*,
and nationalist slogans ("slava ukraini").

These are hidden **even when someone only means a time zone** ("LF EU players", "american guild, NA
times"). In practice, talk that starts from where people are from turns into "<nation> players are
all…", politics or a pile-on far more often than it helps anyone, and TolerableChat's author decided
it isn't worth the channel. Arrange raid times in guild chat or whispers, or switch single words such
as *eu* off in Edit terms if you need them.

Left out on purpose: language names players use to find groups (*english*, *spanish*, *german*…) and
countries whose names are ordinary words (*turkey*, *chad*, *jordan*, *polish*, *chile*…).

### Religion (Timeout)
Religions, denominations, scripture and preaching phrases ("praise jesus", "repent", "deus vult",
"christ is king"). Not because faith is unwelcome, but because public chat sermons and the arguments
they start aren't the game.

### Sexual (Timeout)
Crude sexual words, as whole words only (so "analysis" is fine), including "rape" in the gaming sense
("we raped them"), ERP and OnlyFans. *Whore* and *slut* are here rather than in Bigotry because players
use them about themselves ("loot whore", "I'm such a slut for mounts").

### Bigotry (Mute Forever)
Hostility toward people rather than a topic, so the person is muted:
- **Slurs:** racial, ethnic and anti-LGBT slurs, including spellings with digits and symbols and
  run-together forms, and hate slogans (*sieg heil*, *1488*, *white power*).
- **Open bigotry:** Nazi salutes and admiring euphemisms ("heil", "hitler did nothing wrong",
  "austrian painter"), antisemitic phrases and tropes (*shekels*, *globalists*), mocking names for
  LGBT people, and "go back to your country".
- **Dog whistles:** phrases that look harmless but carry a hateful meaning to those who know them,
  taken from the Dog Whistle Glossary and HurtLex (see Credits in the README). Only rare,
  unambiguous, hateful ones are used. Political insults that some lists call "coded" (*sjw*,
  *cuckservative*, *womxn*) are in Politics instead, with a timeout. If one of these words is used
  innocently where you play, untick just that word in **Edit terms…**.

### Harassment (Timeout)
"kys", "kill yourself", "hang yourself", "drink bleach" and variants. Advice that only means dying in
the game ("don't kill yourself soloing that", "you'll kill yourself pulling that pack") is left alone.
A Timeout rather than Mute Forever: for many players "kys" is everyday banter, and the timeout still
takes the rest of the exchange with it. Set it to Mute Forever on the Filter tab if you'd rather.

### Dead Horses (Hide Only)
Jokes everyone has heard a thousand times: "did someone say [Thunderfury]?", Mankrik and his wife,
Chuck Norris. Not harmful, just tired, so only the message goes and nobody gets a timeout. Any
mention of Mankrik is hidden, questions about his quest included.

### Custom (Timeout)
Your own terms, added with **Edit terms…** or `/tc term add`.

## The other filters

- **Symbol and script spam.** ASCII art, braille-art walls, box drawing, and messages written mostly in
  another alphabet (Chinese, Korean, Cyrillic…). TolerableChat is set up for English-speaking chat.
  A "..." or a "?" is never spam. A word mixing Latin with Cyrillic or Greek look-alike letters is
  spam too: that's how gold sellers dodge filters.
- **Spelling tricks.** Every category sees through them: letters split up (`t-r-u-m-p`), digits and
  symbols for letters (`p0litics`, `7rump`, `b!den`), accents and look-alike letters (`trümp`), starred
  letters (`pol*tics`) and stretched ones (`poliitical`). A tricked spelling gets the same treatment as
  the plain word, Mute Forever included.
- **Meme word mute.** When everyone's posting the same joke, the Overview tab offers the most-shared
  words with "Mute 30 min" and "Mute 4 hours". It's never automatic, and the word comes back by itself.
- **Muted guilds.** Members of any guild whose name contains one of your muted guild words are muted
  by guild. Only what the game shows counts: `/who`, nameplates, mouseover. Guilds whose names contain
  a slur or other Mute Forever word are muted the same way, however the name is spelled. Place names
  never count in a guild's name: a guild called "Texas Raiders" is just a guild. The words are on the
  Guilds tab; remove a word, or make a guild an exception, and its members are shown again.

## What's deliberately left alone

- **Game vocabulary that looks like a filtered word**: RFK is Razorfen Kraul, "trans arcanite" is a
  transmute, *Enslave Demon* and *Black Dragonscale* are a spell and an item, the snowflake mount is
  a mount. Item, spell, NPC, quest and zone names are protected, so a word inside them never hides a
  message (TERMS.md lists the protected names).
- **Language words** used to find groups, and **country names that are ordinary words**.
- **Your guild, party, raid and friends**, your own messages and GM messages: never hidden.
- **Whispers**: not word-filtered by default (tick Whispers on the Filter tab to change that). Muted
  players' whispers are still hidden.

## Changing any of it

- **Filter tab:** one choice per category (Mute Forever, Timeout, Hide Only, Off), the Timeout
  Duration, and where filtering applies.
- **Edit terms…:** search the list, untick single words to switch them off, or add your own: a whole
  word, exact text, text anywhere, all of several parts, or a Lua pattern (the README explains each).
- **[TERMS.md](TERMS.md):** every built-in term, readable outside the game.
- **`/tc test some text`:** shows whether a message would be hidden, by which term, and why.
- **Log tab:** hover any hidden line for the reason; flag it if it shouldn't have been hidden. Right-
  click a name → **Flag message** for something that got through. Flags are how the lists get better.
