# TolerableChat's built-in terms

Generated by `build/build_data.py` from `data/mute-terms.txt` (term list version `0125fd01a7`). Don't edit this page: change the source list and build again.

> **Content warning:** the Bigotry list below contains slurs and hateful phrases. The filter has to know them to hide them.

Everything here can be changed in game: **Filter tab → Edit terms…** lists every term with a tick box to switch it off, and you can add your own. Each category's action (Mute Forever, Timeout, Hide Only, Off) is chosen on the Filter tab. [FILTERS.md](FILTERS.md) explains why each category exists.

There are two kinds of built-in term:

- **Words and phrases** (almost all of them) match as whole words, after spelling tricks are undone: `t.r.u.m.p`, `p0litics`, `7rump` and `trümp` all count, and so does a word with a number stuck to it (`trump2028`). Words of 5 or more letters also match starred (`pol*tics`) and stretched (`poliitical`). No wildcards: `trump` doesn't match inside `trumpet`.
- **Patterns**, listed separately under the few categories that have them, are Lua patterns (a simpler cousin of regular expressions) on the lowercased text, for stems where every ending should count. `%f[%w]` means "at the start of a word", `[a@]` means "a or @", and `+` means "one or more". Lua patterns have no either/or (`a|b`), counts (`{2,3}`) or lookarounds; the README has the full syntax.

In game, **Edit terms…** shows each term's match type next to its category (Whole word, Exact text, Anywhere, All of, Pattern), and hovering a term explains what it matches. When you add a term, the **Match:** button picks its type.
